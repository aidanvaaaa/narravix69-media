# Privacy Policy - Narravix69 Shorts Uploader

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Narravix69 Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the narravix69 accounts to upload their own original short videos to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What it accesses: only the permissions needed to upload videos and read basic profile information (name and account ID) for the narravix69 accounts.

What it stores: access tokens are stored encrypted in GitHub Actions secrets. No other personal data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

YouTube: this tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can revoke access at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Deleting data: revoking the app's access in each platform's settings removes all access. Contact the owner to delete anything else.
